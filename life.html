03 / MY LIFE

My Life.

A story made of places, people, memories, and the next chapter I'm heading toward.

EARLY YEARS

Cagayan

I grew up in Cagayan, carrying memories from childhood that were sometimes good and sometimes difficult. At that age, I wasn't always old enough to understand what everything meant.

ELEMENTARY

Heritage Homes

I started elementary school in Heritage Homes and met a lot of genuinely good people. Some stayed for a long time, some only for a chapter, but each person became part of the story.

FRIENDSHIP

Justin

Justin was there from the beginning. We went through our ups and downs together, and even without being related by blood, we became like brothers—just from different mothers.

FRIENDSHIP

Edaine

Edaine became one of my closest friends too. A lot of people might see her differently, but to me she was family—a sister-like friend and one of the two best friends I could have asked for.

FAMILY

My Mom

My family hasn't always been exactly what I wished it could be, but my mom has remained one of the most important people in my life. She's not perfect, and sometimes promises don't work out, but the biggest thing she gave me is still the biggest thing I have: my life.

NEXT CHAPTER

Going Back to Cagayan

I'm looking forward to going back to Cagayan and seeing a place where the people around me once felt less like strangers and more like family. Things may have changed, and I may have changed too, but I'm ready to see what comes next.
