⚔ Favorite Heroes

My favorite hero is Argus. After him, my other favorite heroes are Terizla, Granger, Clint, and Kagura.

⭐ Favorite Skin

My favorite skin is the Darth Vader collaboration skin. The main reason i like it is because of the effects. The effects are what really make the skin stand out to me and make it more satisfying to use.

🎯 My Playstyle
