📖 My Experience

My history with MLBB since 2021, the golden era, the people, the matches, and the memories.

↓

i started playing mlbb back in 2021, and honestly throughout those years i think that was the golden era of mlbb for me. Everything just felt different back then. The game, the people, the matches, even the way playing the game felt. It was one of those periods where you could make memories without even realizing that you were making them.

Over the years, i've played through different stages of the game and watched it change a lot. Some things got better, some things changed completely, and some things just arent the same anymore. But even with all of that, the older years still stand out to me the most.

A big part of why i remember mlbb so much is because it wasnt only about winning matches. It was also about the random games, the moments that made me laugh, the frustrating matches, the times i actually played really well, and all the little things that happened while playing.

My favorite hero is Argus, followed by Terizla, Granger, Clint, and Kagura. I usually play in a passive-aggressive way. I dont always rush into a fight immediately. I prefer watching what the enemy is doing and waiting for an opening, but once i see one, i can become much more aggressive.

My favorite skin is the Darth Vader collaboration skin, mostly because of its effects. The effects are probably the biggest reason the skin stands out to me, and they make using it feel much more satisfying.

According to my Google Play stats, i've spent 302.8 hours playing Mobile Legends. I also reached Achievement Level 99 with 620 out of 1000 achievement points, placing me in the top 0.125% overall.

Looking back, i dont really think of mlbb as just another game i've played. It's something that has been around for years of my life, and because of that, there's a lot more attached to it than just the game itself.
